Meet Stephanie

Stephanie Dill · Rohnert Park, California

A world beyond the canvas.

Layered cardboard becomes bamboo. A wooden box becomes a miniature town. Stephanie’s work invites a closer look at the animals, places, and small details that give each piece its character.

The artist behind the work

Stephanie Dill is a mixed-media artist based in Rohnert Park, California. Working across paint, paper, cardboard, and found materials, she creates art with texture and dimension — from lifelike pet portraits to layered murals and intricate shadow-box scenes.

She's also part of the Renaissance Healing & Learning Center's "Healing on Horseback" community, and her love of animals shows in every portrait she paints.

Every piece leaves her studio one of a kind: bamboo built from layered cardboard, miniature towns assembled by hand, and portraits that capture not just how a pet looks, but who they are.
